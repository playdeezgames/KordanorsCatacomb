# Larrikin of SPLORR!! ][ - Kordanor's Catacomb

"Hallo! and welcome to this, the next entry in the Dungeon Crawler Jam 2023 GAME JAM!"

A first-person crawl through a nine-by-nine catacomb full of Blobs. Yer quest: find a cup of tea, find a match, light the tea to make it "a lit tea", find a sponge, find some dew, collect the dew with the sponge, then squeeze out the dew onto the lit tea, thus achieving: "Dew a lit tea".

Get it?!? IS PUN! IS FUNNY! LUL

My theme implementation is something of a stretch, but I managed to DEW it!

## Playing

- **Arrows** turn and step forward.
- **Space** or **Enter** chooses: change mode (Navigate, Ground, Inventory, Map), take, use, fight.
- **Escape** backs out.
- On a phone, use the on-screen pad.

Yer game is saved as you go; pick **Continue** on the main menu to carry on.

The dice only count on a six. Köttbullar heal, Sylt Lingon makes you hardier, a KNORVA hat helps you defend, and a Vörda knife helps you hit. A compass tells you which way you face.

## Now in yer browser

This Metaphor started as a VB.NET (MonoGame) jam entry for Windows and Linux in April 2023. In November 2026 it was rebuilt in Odin and WebAssembly so it runs in the browser, with the same rules, screens, drawings and sounds. Saving is new. The old downloads are gone; this browser build replaces them.
